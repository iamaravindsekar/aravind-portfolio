Volunteer Link

Helping students across the USA discover, join, and track volunteering opportunities, and walk away with a credible, verifiable portfolio for college admissions.

Overview

Turning good intentions into a resume line

Volunteer Link empowers students to discover, join, and track volunteering opportunities that align with their interests: community service, environmental causes, social impact projects, and local events, while building a credible volunteering portfolio they can showcase during college and university admissions.

Approach


The client arrived without a clear set of requirements, so the first phase focused on translating conversations into high-level problem statements and opportunity areas, using an Understand → Research → Analyze → Design → Launch approach.

Project objectives


  • Create a simple, user-friendly application with easier information flow.
  • Bridge students and organisations looking for volunteers.
  • Let students earn valid certificates that boost their resumes.
  • Onboard verified NPOs to offer real opportunities inside the app.
  • Offer automated compliance reports with minimal manual effort.

User personas






Major insights


  • Verification is key. Students, schools, and coordinators all seek trust and authenticity.
  • Visibility drives motivation. Dashboards, leaderboards, and certificates reinforce engagement.
  • Communication gaps cause friction. Built-in chat or notifications can bridge this.
  • Institutional adoption matters. Partnering with schools and NGOs increases credibility and reach.
  • Automation reduces drop-off. Auto-logging, reminders, and easy certificate issuance keep users active.

Phased rollout


  • Phase 1: MVP with basic dashboard and certificate flows.
  • Phase 2: Chat support and automated certificate verification.
  • Phase 3: Partnerships with organisations and real-time analytics for all users.
