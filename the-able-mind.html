Mental Health Case Study Mobile App UI/UX

The Able Mind

24/7 mental health support through affordable, anonymous counselling: 15-minute chat therapy, peer groups, and self-help tools built to fit into a busy, stigma-heavy world.

RoleProduct Designer
TimelineMay 2022 – Present
DeliverablesResponsive Mobile App
IndustryMental Health
Overview

Confidential therapy, without the friction

The Able Mind is a digital mental health platform focused on accessible, affordable, and confidential online therapy. It caters primarily to Indian users but reaches a wider audience through text-based counselling, self-help tools, and community features available in multiple Indian languages.

Problem statement

  • Mental health stigma and lack of accessible therapy were barriers for many users in India.
  • Time constraints and language barriers prevented in-person therapy.
  • Few resources existed beyond just access to therapy itself.
  • Users needed confidential, easy-to-use support tools for anxiety, depression, stress, and relationships.

Goals

  • Provide affordable, anonymous, and easy access to professional counsellors.
  • Enable proactive mental health self-care through wellness tools.
  • Foster peer community support within a safe environment.
  • Design an engaging, intuitive experience that maximises retention and impact.
Research

Discovery & information architecture

User interviews reported stigma and time constraints as major barriers. Competitive analysis showed gaps in affordability and anonymity across existing counselling options, and app usage data showed a clear desire for immediate, text-based counselling and easy-to-understand self-help resources.

Navigation was split clearly into Therapy Chat, Wellness Tools (journaling, mood tracking), Community, and Resources, with a simple onboarding flow and a clear free-trial explanation.

Design

Visual & interaction design

  • Calming blues and greens supporting a soothing emotional experience.
  • Large, simple buttons with clear labels for accessibility and ease of use.
  • Multi-language support for inclusivity.
  • Design system built on Google Material Design 3.

Key features

  • Live chat therapy with qualified counsellors, available in multiple languages.
  • Anonymous therapy over text for ongoing communication.
  • Peer support groups to foster community and shared experience.
  • Journaling, mood, anxiety, and habit trackers to help users spot patterns.
  • A resource hub: articles, meditation guides, calming music, mental health assessments.
  • Community sharing for anonymous storytelling.
Outcomes

Metrics & impact

Challenges

  • Encouraging sustained habit formation in mental health self-care.
  • Balancing anonymity with effective therapist–user connection.
  • Addressing diverse linguistic and cultural needs across India.

Evolving the product: V3.5 to V7.3

  • Introduced a user preferences dashboard and a new landing-screen carousel.
  • Added AI chat assistant, community connections, habit tracker, journal, mood tracker, and gift-a-subscription.
  • Retired the Auxie mascot and the lilac colour code in later releases as the brand matured.
