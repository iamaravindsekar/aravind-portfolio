Thaipari

Brand identity and packaging for a cold-pressed, organic oil company, from a name meaning "waves" to cans on a shelf.

RoleBrand & Packaging Designer
DeliverablesLogo, Packaging, Print

CategoryOrganic Food
Brand identity

A name that means "waves"

Thaipari manufactures and sells organic food products, primarily cold-pressed wood oils. The logo was derived from the brand name itself, drawn from the Telugu word for waves. It needed to reflect both purity and the idea of offering organic products, while carrying that sense of movement.

Light blue and green became the primary colours, with golden yellow representing the oil itself, with white and black rounding things out as neutrals. The palette was chosen deliberately to hold up on pastel surfaces, darker backgrounds, and plain white alike.

Packaging

1 litre cylindrical steel cans

Each package pairs a lighter pastel primary with a darker shade of the same hue as a secondary colour. Rather than photographing the raw ingredients, stroke illustrations were used throughout to give the range a more premium, considered feel.

500ml packaging

Aravind took the time to understand our needs, did his own research, and came up with amazing designs, even coordinating directly with the printers when things got confusing."Himabindu Tankala, Co-founder, Thaipari
