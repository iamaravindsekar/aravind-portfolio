Mental Health Case Study Mobile App Web App UI/UX

Cogny Health

A mental wellness marketplace built to bridge India's mental health care gap during the COVID lockdowns, reaching Tier 1–3 cities with therapy that doesn't cost a fortune.

RoleFounding Design Director
TimelineOct 2021 – Feb 2023
DeliverablesWeb, Mobile, Landing Page, Print
IndustryMental Health
Objectives

A market for mental wellness, not a burden

  • Deliver evidence-based mental health support through experiences that build self-awareness, emotional regulation, and positive habits, while bridging the mental health care gap by reaching Tier 1–3 cities.
  • Reach users across a wide age range, from 18 to 60.
  • Design workflows that work equally well on web and mobile.
  • Function like a marketplace for mental wellness, offering therapy across a wide cost range so it never feels like a luxury service.
Kickoff

Whiteboarding the problem space

Before any screens, the team mapped the therapy journey end to end: where patients get stuck, where doctors need support, and where the product could remove friction rather than add features.

Market research

Scope & competitor research

The scope: create workflows that feel native on both web and mobile, and price therapy so it never excludes the people who need it most.

Outcome: at the time, no major player in India's mental health space was acting as a marketplace offering a variety of accessible therapies to a wide user base. That gap became the product's reason to exist.

User research

Personas & empathy mapping

In-depth persona creation and empathy mapping surfaced the emotional challenges and expectations that shaped feature prioritisation.

ShreyaRakesh

App opportunitiesMindfulness, journaling, work-life balance toolsGoal tracking, affordable therapy access, healthy lifestyle plans

Key finding: users needed an affordable solution offering a wide range of therapies, providing emotional relief, healthy coping strategies, and opportunities to reconnect with personal goals.

  • Stress & anxiety management: mindfulness, relaxation, guided breathing, journaling, video calls.
  • Work-life balance coaching for professionals and career guidance for young adults.
  • Anonymous community & support chat groups.
  • Family bonding modules encouraging meaningful interactions.
  • Positive reinforcement through health records and progress tracking.
Design process

Card sorting → IA → wireframes → design system → interface

UI design principles

  • Strong, vibrant colors to evoke positivity and energy.
  • Rounded UI elements to avoid harsh lines.
  • Minimalism with friendly microcopy throughout.
  • An older-style menu drawer to keep the interface familiar for elder users.
  • Accessibility-first design, WCAG 2.1 AA compliant.
