Workativ

Redesigning the site for an agentic AI platform that cuts support costs by up to 60%, in a minimalist neo-brutalist language built to make a technical product feel tangible.

RoleUI/UX Designer
Duration25 days
DeliverablesWebsite, Illustrations, Icons
IndustryTechnology
Overview

Agentic AI, made legible

Workativ positions agentic AI for HR and IT support: automating repetitive queries, integrating with existing HRIS/ITSM systems, and escalating to humans when needed. The product story leans on outcomes like automating up to 80% of repetitive queries and cutting support costs by up to 60%, backed by multi-agent orchestration, a RAG-powered knowledge base, workflow automation, omnichannel delivery across Teams, Slack, and web widgets, live-agent handover, and ROI analytics.

Enterprise trust signals such as ISO/IEC 27001:2013, SOC audits, and GDPR compliance, along with ecosystem anchors like Microsoft Teams, needed to read clearly for IT and HR stakeholders evaluating an enterprise-grade tool.

Plan

  • Create a minimalistic neo-brutalist design language concept.
  • Make the website cleaner and stand out with actual product illustrations.
  • Design every screen with a re-usable, module-based approach.
  • Work closely with developers during implementation sprints.
  • Set up a design QA checklist to ensure pixel-perfect execution.
